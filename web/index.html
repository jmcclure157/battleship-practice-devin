        How to play
        1. Place your 5 ships on Your fleet: hover to preview, click to drop the highlighted ship. Press R or the direction button to turn it. Or click Place randomly.
        2. Press Start battle, then click squares in Enemy waters to fire. The computer fires back after each of your shots.
        3. Sink all 5 enemy ships (Carrier 5, Battleship 4, Cruiser 3, Submarine 3, Destroyer 2) before the computer sinks yours.
